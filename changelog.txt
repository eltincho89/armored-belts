Version: 0.2.4
Date: 2026-08-31
  Bugfixes:
    - Fixed a crash on startup without Space Age installed or enabled
      ("Error in assignID: recipe-category with name 'pressing' does not
      exist"). The 0.2.3 fix hardcoded the "pressing" category, which is a
      Space Age category and doesn't exist on a base-only install; it now
      falls back to plain "crafting" in that case, same as vanilla's own
      belt recipes do without the expansion.

  Bugfixes:
    - Fixed armored belt recipes defaulting to the "crafting" category, which
      made them impossible to craft in the Vulcanus Foundry. They now use
      "pressing", matching vanilla belt recipes, and remain hand-craftable.
